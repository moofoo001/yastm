# YASTM (Yet another Star Trek Mod)

Explore, research, and uphold Federation values in RimWorld.  
Adds a playable United Federation of Planets faction, a Starfleet ideology, roles, uniforms, gear (tricorder, medical sash, combadge), and lore-friendly backstories.

## Features
- **Faction & Ideology**: UFP with a Starfleet doctrine meme. Custom roles (Commanding Officer, Counselor, Chief Medical Officer, Chief Security Officer).  
- **Rituals**: Briefing (LeaderSpeech), Promotion (RoleChange), Shore Leave (DanceParty).  
- **Uniforms**: Department-colored tunics (command gold, operations red, science blue, medical white) + pants.  
- **Gear**: Tricorder (research/learning & medical utility), Medical sash (tending bonuses), Combadge overlay.  
- **Scenarios**: Starfleet landing party & Federation gravship start with proper xenotypes (via Ectos).  
- **Backstories**: Starfleet-themed childhood/adulthood pools per division.

## Requirements
- RimWorld 1.6  
- **Ectos Star Trek Genetics** – xenotypes (optional but recommended)

## Compatibility
- Built for Ideology.  
- Minimal patches; most content is defined directly in XML.  
- Load after RimTrek & Ectos.

## Notes
- Core Leader/Moral Guide are disabled for Starfleet ideologies (roles are replaced by Starfleet equivalents).
- Boots are postponed to a future cosmetic pass.
