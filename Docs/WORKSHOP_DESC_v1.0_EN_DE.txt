EN – Workshop Description (v1.0)
----------------------------------
**Star Trek Factions** brings a Federation-inspired player faction with tailored backstories, uniforms, and a stable landing-party scenario.

**Requires:** RimTrek (Continued). Optional: Ectos (Star Trek Genetics) for xenotypes.

**Highlights**
- Federation player faction (UFP) with lore-friendly backstories.
- Landing Party scenario (5 crew, DropPods) – stable in 1.6.
- Starfleet Tunic with proper worn graphics.
- Briefing & Shore Leave rituals (fallback to vanilla outcomes).
- Balanced values – not OP, playable from day one.

**Load Order**
1. Core + DLCs (Ideology/Biotech if used)
2. Humanoid Alien Races (if used)
3. RimTrek (Continued)
4. Star Trek Factions (this mod)

**0.9 → 1.0 Changelog**
- Fixed thought stage descriptions (no more log warnings).
- Ensured start-items use Cloth stuff (no madeFromStuff warnings).
- Apparel now has wornGraphicPath (visible on pawns).
- Removed empty <degree> in backstories; smeltable flags corrected.
- General XML hygiene & cleanup.
